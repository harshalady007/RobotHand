🕯️ 9/11: THE DAY THE WORLD CHANGED FOREVER 🇺🇸

8:46 AM. A normal Tuesday. Then the world stopped: 19 hijackers, 4 planes, 2,977 lives lost. 👇

✈️ 8:46 AM Flight 11 hits the North Tower, 9:03 AM Flight 175 hits the South Tower, 9:37 AM Flight 77 hits the Pentagon (Miller Center timeline)
🛡️ Flight 93 never reached its target: passengers fought back and it crashed in a Pennsylvania field at about 10:02 AM
🌍 Oct 7, 2001: US-led strikes on Afghanistan begin. Nov 19, 2001: the TSA is created and air travel changes for good (US Navy, TSA)
