💥 One explosion. Its shockwave circled the Earth three times. 🌍

The TSAR BOMBA: the most powerful nuclear weapon ever detonated.

☢️ 30 Oct 1961: the Soviet Union tested it over a frozen island in the Arctic (Novaya Zemlya).
📉 Designed for 100 megatons, cut to 50 to reduce fallout. Still 3,000+ times the Hiroshima bomb.
☁️ The mushroom cloud climbed ~64 km, the shockwave knocked the bomber down hundreds of metres, and windows shattered as far away as Finland.

Its designer Andrei Sakharov later became a loud voice for limits on nuclear testing.
