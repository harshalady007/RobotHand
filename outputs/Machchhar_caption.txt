✈️ CAPTAIN SMIT MACHCHHAR 🇮🇳: THE PILOT WHO REFUSED TO GIVE UP

Stabbed. Bleeding. A jet in a dive. One Indian captain kept fighting. 👇

✈️ Sept 30, 2026: Flydubai FZ1073 (Dubai → Tel Aviv) plunged thousands of feet after the captain was attacked in the cockpit, allegedly by his own co-pilot (AP, Wikipedia)
🚪 Badly hurt, Capt. Machchhar still released the cockpit door from inside, so passengers and crew could overpower the attacker (Reuters, Times of Israel)
🛬 The jet landed safely in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia and everyone survived. PM Modi told him his "courage, patience, and presence of mind" saved many lives (AP)
